Welcome! I'm thrilled to finally launch my personal space on the web.

As software developers, we spend an enormous amount of time reading documentation, debugging elusive race conditions, and exploring new libraries and architectures. Over the years, I realized that the best way to retain deep technical knowledge is to **document and teach it**.

### What to Expect Here

This blog is where I will share:

1. **Practical Engineering Tutorials**: Real-world breakdowns of building fullstack web apps, optimizing database queries, and designing scalable APIs.
2. **Architecture Breakdowns**: System design patterns, microservices vs. modular monoliths, and distributed systems.
3. **Productivity & Dev Tooling**: Setups, command-line shortcuts, and automation workflows that accelerate development.
4. **Project Retrospectives**: Honest post-mortems of projects I build, discussing what worked and what failed.

### How this Blog is Built

This blog is built as a static site hosted for free on **GitHub Pages**, leveraging standard Jekyll templating:

```markdown
- Blazing fast performance (Zero client-side JS overhead for reading)
- 100% free hosting with automated deployment
- Markdown-first workflow for frictionless writing
```
